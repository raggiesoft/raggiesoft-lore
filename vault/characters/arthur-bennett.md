# Arthur Bennett

Arthur Bennett is a key figure originating from the `/luna-leo` narrative. Raised with a strong sense of independence, Arthur grew up completely unaware of his extended family on his father's side. 

## The Bennett Schism of 1978
Before Arthur was born, his father and his uncle had a catastrophic falling out over a failed family business venture and a disputed inheritance. The argument was so volatile that the two brothers permanently severed all ties. Incredibly, both branches of the family remained in Virginia Beach, VA, but actively hid the existence of the other from their children. They could pass each other at the grocery store and never know they were blood relatives. 

Because of this, Arthur grew up never knowing he had a paternal cousin named [Sam Bennett](/vault/characters/sam-bennett.md), nor does he know about Sam's descendants (including Jack and Sarah).
