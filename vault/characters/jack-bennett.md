# Jack Bennett

Jack Bennett is the son of [Sam Bennett](/vault/characters/sam-bennett.md) and the brother of [Sarah Bennett](/vault/characters/sarah-bennett.md). He is part of the Bennett family branch that remained completely separated from his second cousin [Arthur Bennett](/vault/characters/arthur-bennett.md) due to the Bennett Schism of 1978.

Jack grew up in Virginia Beach, VA, unaware of the broader family tree that had been hidden from him by his grandfather.
