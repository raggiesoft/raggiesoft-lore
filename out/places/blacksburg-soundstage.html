The Blacksburg Soundstage & Progress Street NE Sanctuary

Home base of The Stardust Engine in Blacksburg—encompassing Aunt Martha's farmhouse on Progress Street NE and the private warehouse soundstage.

Gazetteer & Worldbuilding Notes

From August 1985 onward, siblings Ryan, Cassidy, and Holly O'Connell and identical twin cousins Evan and Tyler Wright made their lifelong home together at Aunt Martha's farmhouse on Progress Street NE in Blacksburg, Virginia—never living in campus dormitories.

Following the 1994 emancipation of their master recordings and the founding of Engine Room Records, LLC in-universe, the family established the private Blacksburg Soundstage warehouse complex. Inside its 1G industrial bay—outfitted with scissor-lifts, steel rigging cables, custom stepless wheelchair ramps, and zero-threshold control rooms—The Stardust Engine produced their 1996 short film Escape Velocity (Ad Astra) and recorded their post-emancipation catalog on their own schedule (strictly after Ryan's 10:00 AM daily floor).
