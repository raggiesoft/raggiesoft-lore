Highcliff Mansion & The Coolidge Hill Estate

Gilded Age coastal Bellevue Avenue estate in Newport and historic residential sanctuary in West Cambridge.

Stories, Characters & Bands Set Here

■ Featured In Book Series

  • The Quantum Directive: The Caretakers
  • Meredith

● Connected Characters

  • David Vance

Gazetteer & Worldbuilding Notes

Highcliff is a massive, privately-owned Gilded Age mansion located on Bellevue Avenue in Newport, Rhode Island. It sits on prime real estate north of Ruggles Avenue, ensuring it is fully serviced by OSTA Route 67 from the Newport Gateway Center, bypassing the morning service delays south of "Hello Queen" (Salve Regina) University.

When Quantum expands its footprint by opening a massive Research & Development Center in Cambridge, Massachusetts , Cassandra is temporarily relocated to the East Coast to oversee the new facility. Quantum specifically taps her for the role because she is from New England (Aquidneck Island) and possesses the precise executive operational experience required to launch the site.

Upon receiving her official deployment orders in early 2008, Cassandra purchases the Coolidge Hill Estate—a massive, 19th-century colonial revival mansion in West Cambridge. During the week, Cassandra, David, Liz, and Casey live here. This allows Cassandra to maintain a reasonable, localized commute to the Quantum facility using the BSTA (Bay State Transit Authority, commonly referred to in-universe as "The B").

However, Cassandra also wanted a permanent East Coast stronghold. When Highcliff quietly hit the market as a pocket listing, she executed a hostile real-estate acquisition overnight. She submitted a massive all-cash bid the night before the New York hedge fund managers even noticed the listing. The seller almost immediately accepted her offer because they recognized the "Vance" name and knew Cassandra was originally born and raised in Newport, Rhode Island. By the time the other millionaires—who just wanted another mansion—saw the house was on the market the very next day, the seller had already verbally accepted Cassandra's offer and was in the process of writing up the contract to send to Cassandra's legal team to finalize the deal.

While Highcliff serves as her weekend retreat when she escapes Cambridge, she essentially weaponizes her wealth to secure the pack: she relocates both the Rhode Island Delaney family and the Virginia Beach Delaney family into the massive estate as their permanent, full-time residence. By consolidating both households under the Highcliff roof, she ensures they have a massive, fully accessible place to live where the bills, taxes, and maintenance are permanently covered by her.

Cassandra maintains this East Coast lifestyle until 2012, when the Cambridge facility is fully operational and local leadership is trained. She is subsequently recalled back to her primary residence in Redmond, Washington—a massive corporate maneuver that directly sets up her arc to become Quantum's CEO in 2013.

Highcliff is a sprawling, U-shaped Italian Renaissance villa built around a massive central courtyard. It is shielded from the public eye by thick privet hedges and massive wrought-iron gates.

Its most famous architectural feature is the Solarium—a massive, heated, glass-enclosed indoor botanical garden. During winter visits, the pack completely repurposes the Solarium. Instead of using the dozens of historic, opulent individual bedrooms, they drag massive mattresses into the center of the heated glass room, creating a colossal, overlapping "puppy pile" where all of them sleep together directly under the winter stars.

Much like The Breakers, Highcliff was an absolute marvel of modern engineering when it was built. It featured technology that was decades ahead of its time, making it incredibly fitting that it would eventually be purchased by a modern-day tech CEO.

The original Gilded Age construction included a massive, state-of-the-art subterranean boiler heating system, early electrical wiring routed through gas pipes, and an original electric passenger elevator servicing the first and second floors. Cassandra was drawn to the estate not just for its beauty, but because it was the 19th-century equivalent of a Quantum tech campus.

The Coolidge Hill Estate is Cassandra Vance's primary East Coast residence, utilized while she serves as the Executive Vice President of Operations overseeing the Quantum NERD (New England Research & Development) Center.

Location & Architecture: A massive, heavily wooded 19th-century colonial revival mansion located on Coolidge Hill in West Cambridge. It provides the sweeping, secluded estate feel of her Newport property (Highcliff) while keeping her seamlessly embedded in the Cambridge transit grid.

The ADA Retrofit: Before moving in, the Vance Family Trust executed a flawless, historical-board-approved ADA integration. Casey designed a commercial-grade elevator shaft completely enclosed within a carriage house addition, preserving the original facade and matching the historic red brick and trim. This ensures Cassandra and the other wheelchair users in the pack have unrestricted access to the entire compound.

The Inner Circle: The Delaney family are lifelong friends who have known Cassandra and her family, including Liz, their entire lives.

Full-Time Guardianship: To ensure that a home owned by Cassandra Vance is never left empty or vulnerable, members of the Delaney family live full-time across her various properties.

Unrestricted Access: The Delaneys hold "Sovereign" clearance within the Trust's architecture. They are explicitly permitted to show up unannounced to any of the homes Cassandra owns, operating as a trusted, permanent extension of the pack's domestic perimeter.

Despite possessing extreme wealth, Cassandra and David prioritize the use of public transit. The commute from Coolidge Hill to the Quantum NERD Center is highly optimized:

  1. The Mount Auburn Roll: Cassandra and David wheel out of the Coolidge Hill compound and navigate the paved sidewalks a short distance to the bus stop on Mt. Auburn Street.
  2. The BSTA Bus: They board either the BSTA Route 71 or Route 73, utilizing the low-floor accessibility and dedicated transit priority systems to bypass Cambridge traffic.
  3. The Harvard Tunnel Transfer: The bus bypasses the weather completely by descending into the historic Harvard Square Underground Busway.
  4. The Red Line to Kendall/MIT: From the subterranean busway, they take the elevator down to the Red Line platforms. They board an Inbound train for a short, two-stop ride to the Kendall/MIT Station, emerging via the station's accessible elevators directly beside the Quantum NERD building.
