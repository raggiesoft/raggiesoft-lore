3. Academic Buildings & The Engineering Quad

CPI Course Code Standard: All CPI courses across every narrative strictly use a 4-letter department prefix + 3-digit course number (e.g., ASTR 101, PHYS 102, HIST 101, HIST 115, ENGL 102, MATH 112, HLTH 201, PSYC 101, JAPN 101).

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | | Engineering Quad (Whittemore / Hancock / Randolph / Goodwin) | The Engineering Quad | Northwest of Commonwealth Green, reached via steep Ironstone steps and high-grade ADA switchback ramps. | Core territory for CPI's engineering and computer science programs. | | Whittemore / Durham Hall | Faraday Hall | Electrical & Systems Engineering | Houses the 3rd-Floor Systems & Control Lab where John works on his senior capstone telemetry arrays in Before the Distance. | | Randolph / Goodwin Hall | Joule Hall | Mechanical, Aerospace & Materials Engineering | Houses the wind tunnels, materials foundries, and structural truss labs on the Engineering Quad. | | McBryde / Keystone CS Wing | Kelvin Hall | Computer Science & Symbolic Logic | Houses the Unix/mainframe terminal labs and discrete math lecture halls. | | McBryde Hall (General Lecture Amphitheater) | Archer Hall | General Undergraduate Lecture Hall | Across Commonwealth Green from Callahan Hall; houses the large fluorescent-lit lecture halls where HLTH 201 ("Dimensions of Human Sexuality") is taught in Paige and HIST 115 ("History of Ancient Rome") is taught by Dr. Gable in Aubrie. | | Robeson / Hahn Hall (Science Complex) | Copernicus Hall ( & The Science Complex) | Physics, Astronomy & Earth Sciences | Older brutalist concrete/Ironstone science building with a steep, tiered 200-seat amphitheater lecture hall (where wheelchair seating is restricted to the top rear row before SSD intervention). Houses ASTR 101 (Introduction to Astronomy & Astrophysics) and PHYS 102 (University Physics II) in both Paige and Aubrie, plus the CPI Geological Sciences seismograph. | | Major Williams / Lane Hall | Carter Hall | History & Humanities | Older brick building on the upper quad reached via a grueling, steep winding concrete incline; houses Dr. Gable's HIST 101: Western Civilization I class where Jordan meets Sarah Braithwaite in Aubrie. | | Satellite Professional & Continuing Education Campus (Simmons Rd NE, Christiansburg) | Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute, Christiansburg Campus (CPI Christiansburg Campus, colloquially "the Law School") | CPI School of Law, CPI School of Nursing, Undergraduate Overflow Sections, & Evening / Working-Adult / Graduate Education | Located off Simmons Road NE in Christiansburg (served permanently by Blue Ridge Transit Route 4). Originated as the CPI School of Law and was later joined by the CPI School of Nursing (prompting the formal name CPI Christiansburg Campus), though students, Nursing majors, BRT riders, and Christiansburg locals universally call the entire site "the Law School" ("Where's your class tonight?" "At the Law School."). Also houses general academic classrooms for overflow sections of main-campus courses not requiring specialized Blacksburg labs (business, accounting, finance, economics, math, English/composition, political science, psychology, general education, information systems) and heavy late-afternoon/evening working-adult, continuing-education, and classroom-oriented master's programs (100% CPI classes—never an inferior branch or community college). From 2010 through Summer 2012 (while Falling Branch was closed for VDOT redevelopment), part of the Christiansburg Campus parking lot served as the Temporary VDOT Park & Ride and temporary western terminus of Route 4 (signed 4 CPI CBURG—never CPI LAW SCHOOL). |
