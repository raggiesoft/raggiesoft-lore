Story Origins & Sanctuary Lore

Scoring Runebreaker & The 16-Bit Digital Realm

Mana Canvas bridges RaggieSoft's Runebreaker / Dream Kingdom narrative universe (books.raggiesoft.com/runebreaker) with Engine Room Records' audio engineering. Every release is structured like a golden-era 1990s 16-bit 16-bit Super Kinoka RPG soundtrack brought to life with a full rock orchestra.

From SPC-700 Chiptunes to the 1993 Live Concert Stage

Following the 1992 release of the ambient 16-bit Runebreaker soundtrack chronicling Ocea's journey into the digital realm, Mana Canvas took the score on the road for 1993's Runebreaker Live—translating 8-channel SPC-700 melodies into full-bandwidth progressive rock instrumentation and culminating in a 6-minute arena singalong of "The Grand Decree."
