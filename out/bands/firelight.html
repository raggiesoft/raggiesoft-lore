Firelight

1980s Fantasy-Adventure Movie Soundtracks, Synth-Pop Themes & Symphonic Rock

The official 1980s motion picture soundtrack ensemble behind The Silver Gauntlet of Aethel—Michael P. Ragsdale's love letter to 1980s fantasy-adventure cinema. Anchored by its flagship main theme "A Sister's Love" (heavily inspired by classic 1984 widescreen fantasy cinema synth-pop themes), Firelight fuses shimmering 1980s analog synth-pop arpeggios and soaring melodic hooks with widescreen fantasy orchestration.

Story Origins & Sanctuary Lore

A Love Letter to 1980s Fantasy-Adventure Cinema & Limahl's "NeverEnding Story"

The Silver Gauntlet of Aethel was created as Michael P. Ragsdale's personal love letter to 1980s fantasy-adventure movies. When composing the saga's signature main theme, "A Sister's Love (Main Theme)," Michael took very heavy inspiration from classic 1984 widescreen fantasy cinema synth-pop themes—marrying propulsive 1980s synth-pop arpeggios, shimmering electronic basslines, and an unforgettable, sky-scraping chorus that captures the wonder, peril, and unbreakable sibling bond between twins Kaelan and Kaela.

The 1987 Truncated Theatrical LP vs. The 2017 3-Disc "Leviathan Cut" Soundtrack

In-universe, Firelight's original 1987 release—The Silver Gauntlet of Aethel (Theatrical Soundtrack)—was heavily edited down to fit a single vinyl LP, reflecting the studio-mandated truncated theatrical cut of the film that omitted the final two eras in favor of a tidy "happily ever after." Thirty years later, in 2017, Engine Room Records released the definitive 3-disc "Leviathan Cut" Original Motion Picture Soundtrack across Book I: The Unwinding Path, Book II: The Shadow's Heart, and Book III: The Dawn of the Twins—restoring the complete four-era mythic arc.

Companion Literary Saga: The Silver Gauntlet of Aethel

Every movement of Firelight's soundtrack score pairs directly with the four-era fantasy saga The Silver Gauntlet of Aethel on RaggieSoft Book Reader (books.raggiesoft.com/aethel), while all four soundtrack albums and full lyrics stream in The Listening Room at music.raggiesoft.com/firelight.
