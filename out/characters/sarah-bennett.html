Full Storyline Arc & Later Era Milestones

Reveal Later Storyline Milestones & Archival Character Notes (Contains Spoilers Beyond Era 1)

Sarah Bennett is the daughter of Sam Bennett and the sister of Jack Bennett. She was born and raised in Virginia Beach, VA.

Like the rest of her immediate family, Sarah is entirely unaware of the Bennett Schism of 1978 and does not know of the existence of her second cousin, Arthur Bennett.

Sarah's name and identity later became the namesake for a deployment automation script (sarah-deploy.sh) within the RaggieSoft server infrastructure.
