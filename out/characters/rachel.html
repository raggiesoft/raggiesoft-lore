Full Storyline Arc & Later Era Milestones

Reveal Later Storyline Milestones & Archival Character Notes (Contains Spoilers Beyond Era 1)

Rachel's early years in the Dojang were shattered by a profound betrayal of trust from an authority figure. As a young martial arts student, she was subjected to the cruelty of Master Curtis, an instructor who weaponized his rank to terrorize his students.

Despite her young age—only eight years old at the time—Rachel possessed a fierce, innate courage. She was the one who found her voice to call Curtis out, telling him directly that he was mean. In retaliation, Curtis took a brutal, adult-only pain-tolerance ritual and turned it against the children. He deliberately stepped on Rachel, her ten-year-old brother, Michael, and a six-year-old boy named Drew, using his full adult weight to crush them.

The sheer physics of the act caused devastating physical trauma, including torn fascia, forcing Rachel and Michael to be bedridden for months. The incident left Rachel with deep psychological scars, manifesting as selective mutism—a defense mechanism against the adults who had failed to protect them.

Yet, in the dim light of the pediatric trauma bay, Curtis’s attempt to break them had the opposite effect. It forged an impenetrable wall between Rachel, Michael, Drew, and their closest friends. Her voice may have been weaponized against her, but her spirit remained unbroken.
