Full Storyline Arc & Later Era Milestones

Reveal Later Storyline Milestones & Archival Character Notes (Contains Spoilers Beyond Era 1)

Jack Bennett is the son of Sam Bennett and the brother of Sarah Bennett. He is part of the Bennett family branch that remained completely separated from his second cousin Arthur Bennett due to the Bennett Schism of 1978.

Jack grew up in Virginia Beach, VA, unaware of the broader family tree that had been hidden from him by his grandfather.
