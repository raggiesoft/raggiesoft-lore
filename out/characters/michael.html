Full Storyline Arc & Later Era Milestones

Reveal Later Storyline Milestones & Archival Character Notes (Contains Spoilers Beyond Era 1)

Michael was ten years old when his life—and his perspective on authority—was irrevocably altered on the mats of the Dojang. Testing for his brown belt alongside his eight-year-old sister, Rachel, Michael was subjected to the cruelty of Master Curtis.

When Curtis retaliated against Rachel, Michael—along with a six-year-old boy named Drew—was caught in the crossfire. The trauma of being crushed beneath the weight of a grown man left Michael with severe physical injuries, including partially torn abdominal fascia that required months of grueling recovery. He lost the freedom of his fall, anchored to a bedpan and homebound tutoring. But even as he suffered, he witnessed the unimaginable: little Drew being rushed to emergency surgery to save his life.

However, the physical devastation was met with a fierce emotional resolve. In the pediatric trauma bay, clutching his sister's hand under the harsh fluorescent lights, Michael realized that the adults who had allowed this to happen were ruined, but the children were not. Curtis’s attempt to break them only succeeded in forging an unbreakable wall between Michael, Rachel, Drew, and the rest of their cohort.
