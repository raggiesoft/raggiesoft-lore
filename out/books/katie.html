Complete Series Synopsis

Katie & Nathaniel follows the five-decade journey of autistic fraternal twins Katie and Nathaniel ("Nate") Prairie (born April 8, 1985) across Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Richmond, and Washington, D.C. From secret afternoon cartoon passwords, summer camp at Virginia Dominion University alongside Michael and Rachel Ragsdale, the Blizzard of '96, and getting their first compact powerchairs at sixteen, to welcoming Heather and Wesley into their lifelong Virginia household at Graduation 2003, walking out of Roosevelt Rehabilitation Center in Fishersville in July 2007, launching the Ideas for CVTA transit blog, rescuing cousins Brittany and Chelsea into their six-person Alaskan King home, and rising from Kempsville City Council to the Virginia House of Delegates, the Senate of Virginia, and the United States Senate, Nate and Katie prove that nobody in their family ever gets left behind.
