Complete Series Synopsis

Fed up after weeks of petty fights inside his abusive girlfriend's rundown trailer off Route 13, twenty-three-year-old Nolan Lennox (5'7") seized his chance to escape when she impulsively dragged him toward a day-of cross-country bus trip without even calling forty-eight hours ahead for a wheelchair lift—stopping her in his wheelchair before the Norfolk Pathfinder ticket counter, where fellow passengers pushed her back after she violently smacked him and helped sobbing Nolan ride home to Emerald Creek for his kid sister Gwen's eighteenth birthday and their dying grandfather. Grandpa Walter held on to see Gwen turn eighteen—where an overjoyed Gwen accepted Nolan's invite to live together—before passing away the next morning, leaving Nolan to shoulder probate, funeral paperwork, selling Grandpa's house, and sharing his tiny rectangular one-person Studio 2B with six-foot-one Gwen until an amber rift pulls them barefoot on Lofstrand forearm crutches into twin-moon Vaeloria to heal The Great Drift and earn their glowing-archway home forever.
