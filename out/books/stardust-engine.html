Complete Series Synopsis

The forty-year story of The Stardust Engine (1978–2018), told in third-person limited perspective through the eyes of lead guitarist Ryan O'Connell—from the sonic frequencies of Blacksburg High School and Aunt Martha Wright's farmhouse on Progress Street NE, through the 1990 highway crash at Mile Marker 98 and the 1992 Friction catastrophe in Los Angeles, to the 2016 homecoming concert inside The Crucible and the 2018 Nine-Figure Refusal.
