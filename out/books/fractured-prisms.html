Complete Series Synopsis

Fractured Prisms is a mind-bending, abstract thriller that shatters conventional narrative. As the protagonists navigate a world of fragmented realities and shifting truths, they must piece together the vibrant, dangerous shards of their past to understand their future. It is a story of perception, memory, and the colorful, often painful nature of existence.
