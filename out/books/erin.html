Complete Series Synopsis

An official five-song narrative story published by Ryan and Cassidy O'Connell of The Stardust Engine across Progress Street (2004) and Lost Sounds (2007). At Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI) in Blacksburg, Virginia, fraternal twins John and Erin navigate the steep Ironstone hills of campus in their power wheelchairs—forging an unbreakable bond of sibling sanctuary across the rain-slicked curbs of Commonwealth Mall and the limestone halls of campus.
