Complete Series Synopsis

Samantha is a heart-pounding, relentless thriller that begins with a terrifying abduction and quickly transforms into a story of absolute, unbreakable sibling devotion. When Samantha refuses to let her sister be taken alone, she sets off a chain of events that will test their courage, their resilience, and their desperate determination to survive against impossible odds.
